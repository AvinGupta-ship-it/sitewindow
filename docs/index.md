# SiteWindow

SiteWindow evaluates the landscape of hydrogen binding sites in defective, curved and
oxygen-functionalized sp² carbon against the thermodynamic, kinetic and stoichiometric
constraints of a storage cycle. The scientific program, components, data sources, benchmarks and
acceptance criteria are specified in the research manual and the linked specifications.

## Components

| Subpackage | Content |
|---|---|
| `sitewindow.theory` | Langmuir and dissociative occupancy, swing functions, entropy models, optimum and threshold energies, deliverable-capacity functional, variational bound |
| `sitewindow.kinetics` | Polanyi–Wigner kinetics, Redhead relations, linear-ramp desorption, kinetic admissibility |
| `sitewindow.defects` | McKinley–Feshbach knock-on cross sections with Debye-model smearing, defect rate equations, Raman defect relations |
| `sitewindow.experimental` | NIST ISODB mirror loader, carbon selection, unit normalization |
| `sitewindow.atomistic` | structure builders for every site class, site descriptors, calculator adapters (tblite T2, GPAW T1, MACE T3), relaxation and single-point workflows with content-addressed caching, landscape construction, Tier-1 anchors and convergence, sticking and recombination barriers by constrained relaxation and climbing-image NEB, pairwise physisorption potentials |
| `sitewindow.landscape` | site-class catalogue: recipes, enumerated sites, multiplicities, mass changes and hydrogen configurations per tier |
| `sitewindow.schema` | pydantic models of every record type with provenance, uncertainty and physical range checks; flat record tables |
| `sitewindow.io` | Parquet tables carrying the schema version; SHA-256 manifests |
| `sitewindow.validation` | benchmark runners reading thresholds from the registry |
| `sitewindow.workflows` | command-line interface |

## Interpretation of outputs

- `theory_window_tables`: for each window and entropy model, the optimal site energy ε* (kJ mol⁻¹ per H₂), the window efficiency η (maximum fraction of site capacity deliverable), the molecular-channel bound D_max (wt%) for a monolayer on 2630 m² g⁻¹, and the heterogeneity threshold ε_c for a −5.8 kJ mol⁻¹ reference site.
- `validation_report.json`: one record per benchmark with predicted and reference values, the metric, and pass/fail against the registry threshold; `passed: null` marks runners that report without a pass criterion at the current tier.
- `landscape_raw_<tier>.parquet`: one row per energy record of the atomistic landscape (binding energies per H with the ½H₂ and the free-atom references, sequential binding energies, defect formation energies) with tier, engine, settings hash, descriptors of the site carbon and provenance; `landscape_configurations_<tier>.parquet` holds the relaxed structures (extended XYZ) and spin multiplicities, `landscape_raw_<tier>_excluded.csv` the non-converged configurations with reasons, and `landscape_manifest_<tier>.json` the calculator settings, reference energies and file hashes.
- `tier3/`: the same landscape and barrier tables computed with the Tier-3 MACE potential, kept apart from the Tier-2 tables of the tier directory.
- `barriers_<tier>.parquet`: one row per pathway with the sticking barrier (from the free H atom and the relaxed substrate) or the recombination barrier (from the relaxed adsorbed state) of its primary method, the constrained scan for sticking and the NEB for recombination, the other method when the preferred one has not located the saddle and the scan when neither has, with `converged` recording whether that method located a saddle; `barrier_profiles_<tier>.json` holds every method's result with the energy along the coordinate, the transition and final structures, the final-state label of each band and the reaction energies; `barriers_manifest_<tier>.json` the settings and file hashes.
- `physisorption_<tier>.parquet`: strongest H₂ site energy (eV per H₂, negative for binding) of each physisorption geometry for each pair-potential parameter set, classically and with the Feynman–Hibbs correction at 77, 233 and 298 K; the full tier adds the sites adjacent to the epoxide, hydroxyl, carbonyl-pair and carboxyl groups, whose oxygens interact through the OPLS-AA oxygen parameters of their group type.
- `tier1/tier1_anchor_basal_<n>x<n>.json`: Tier-1 basal-monomer anchor with the PBE and D3 parts of the binding energy reported separately, the relaxation record and the comparison with benchmark B1; `tier1_convergence_basal_<n>x<n>.json` the convergence variants of acceptance criterion B1.
- `tier1/anchors/<class>__<substrate>__<configuration>.json`: one record per Tier-1 anchor of the registry `data/registry/tier1_anchors.yaml` (plane-wave, LCAO and D3 energies, binding energies with both references as PBE and PBE-D3(BJ), the defect formation energy where the class defines a pristine reference, descriptors, relaxation records, the Tier-2 pre-relaxation record and the relaxed structure); `tier1/anchors_tier1.parquet` the Tier-1 landscape table rebuilt from these records after every anchor, with sequential binding energies where the parent anchor exists, and `tier1/anchors_manifest_tier1.json` the per-anchor wall times.
- `tier2/smearing_extrapolation.json`: the electronic-temperature study of the Tier-2 protocol (free energy, internal energy and zero-temperature extrapolation of the far-hydrogen offset and of the basal-monomer, monovacancy and epoxide binding energies by cell and temperature), with `smearing_binding.csv` and `smearing_far_hydrogen.csv` as flat tables.
- `tier2/stone_wales_cell_series.json`: the Tier-2 cell series of the Stone–Wales formation energy and rotated-bond monomer binding energy (6×6 to 12×12 cells, extrapolated protocol) and the out-of-plane buckling test of the clean defect.
- Verification-tier outputs (`data/processed/verify/`) use reduced configurations and are not evidence for publication claims.

## Running the atomistic workflows

```bash
sitewindow landscape build --tier verify            # GFN2-xTB landscape of the verify-tier class set
sitewindow landscape physisorption --tier verify    # pair-potential physisorption set
sitewindow landscape barriers --tier verify --method constrained_scan,ci_neb   # barriers along the designated pathways
sitewindow tier2 smearing                           # electronic-temperature study of the Tier-2 protocol
mpirun -np 4 gpaw python --module sitewindow.workflows.cli tier1 anchor --supercell 5   # Tier-1 anchor (conda environment)
mpirun -np 5 gpaw python --module sitewindow.workflows.cli tier1 anchors                # Tier-1 anchor set over the registry, resumable
mpirun -np 4 gpaw python --module sitewindow.workflows.cli tier1 converge --supercell 5 # convergence series
```

Tier-1 runs use the `gpaw python` wrapper under `mpirun`; every rank computes and rank 0 writes. Results are cached per structure, calculator settings and spin state, so interrupted runs resume from the cache.

## Reference hardware

The reference workstation for this program is one Apple Silicon machine:

- Processor: Apple M4, 10 cores (4 performance and 6 efficiency cores), arm64.
- Memory: 24 GB unified memory.
- Operating system: macOS 15.6.1 (Darwin 24.6.0).
- Package environment: Python 3.12.16 in the project virtual environment (`.venv`) with the
  `xtb`, `bayes`, `uq` and `dev` extras; tblite 0.8.0 provides the Tier-2 GFN2-xTB engine.
- Tier-1 density-functional engine: GPAW 26.7.0 from conda-forge in the `sitewindow` conda
  environment defined by `environment.yml` (Open MPI 5.0.11, OpenMP, libxc 7.1.2, libvdwxc, FFTW,
  ScaLAPACK, ELPA, PAW datasets `gpaw_data` 1.0.1), with D3(BJ) dispersion from `dftd3-python`
  1.7.0. GPAW is the active Tier-1 engine; the PySCF fallback is not installed. The installation
  was verified by LCAO (dzp) calculations of H and H₂ and a plane-wave calculation of a periodic
  graphene cell.

## Known limitations at this version

- GFN2-xTB in tblite samples the Γ point only. Graphene cells whose size is a multiple of 3 fold the Dirac point onto Γ and the others miss it, so at 300 K electronic temperature the basal-monomer chemisorption energy oscillates by up to 1 eV between adjacent cell sizes and the verify-tier value reported earlier (−0.21 eV in a 4×4 cell) was dominated by this finite-size error rather than by the method. The energy tblite reports under Fermi smearing is the Mermin free energy F = E − TS. Under the first smearing protocol (3000 K, 12×12 and 9×9 sheets) the free energies of the two cell families agree within 0.1 eV for sp² sites and the zero-temperature extrapolation E₀ = F + TS/2 does not improve that agreement; at 6000 K the extrapolated energies agree across the cell families within 0.03 eV for the basal monomer (−0.80 eV), the first dangling-bond hydrogen of the monovacancy (−3.28 eV) and the hydrogen beside an epoxide (−1.23 eV). The Tier-2 protocol is therefore E₀ at 6000 K, with 0.03 eV as the numerical uncertainty of Tier-2 chemisorption energies (the manual's 0.05 eV target is met); both Tier-2 landscape tables are recomputed under the new protocol (the full tier in `data/processed/full`: 125 configurations, no exclusions); against the first-protocol full-tier table the sheet sp² and tube records move by −0.08 to +0.03 eV, the vacancy dangling bonds by up to −0.11 eV and the defect formation energies by +0.02 to +0.11 eV, while three records (the fourth hydrogen of the 5-8-5 divacancy and the first two hydrogens of the (8,8) tube vacancy) moved by 1 to 4 eV because the two runs relaxed into different minima; their first-protocol minima are being re-relaxed under the extrapolated protocol so that the lower minimum enters the table, and until then those three records carry that caveat. The Tier-1 PBE value lies 0.09 eV below the diffusion Monte Carlo reference, consistent with the Tier-0 offset the manual anticipates.
- The Debye-smeared knock-on model reproduces the static onset and the 100 keV cross section within a factor of two but underestimates the published 80 keV tail by about two orders of magnitude; alternative smearing kinematics remain to be compared.
- Corpus inference is limited to data harmonization; isosteric heats and adsorption-energy-distribution inversion are not yet implemented.
- The single-site Lennard-Jones H₂–C potential cannot satisfy benchmarks B3 and B4 jointly: the literature parameter sets underestimate the CCSD(T) coronene binding by about 1.4 kJ mol⁻¹, and the set calibrated to B3 places the 0.7 nm slit-pore site above the isosteric-heat interval of B4. Both parameterizations are carried as model-form alternatives; their spread (about 45 % of the site energy) is the physisorption model-form uncertainty until an anisotropic or many-body potential is adopted.
- MACE-MP-0 (Tier 3) binds hydrogen about 1 eV more strongly than Tier 2 and PBE on sp² sites of the verify set (basal monomer −1.85 eV against −0.82 eV) and about 0.4 eV more strongly on the dangling bonds of the monovacancy; the offset is class-dependent and Tier-3 values enter only through the per-class calibration of C3. Tier-2 barriers referenced to the separated reactants cannot be computed under a smearing protocol, because the hydrogen atom far above the sheet then lies about 0.5 eV above the separated fragments in every cell (and 0.8 eV in the 9×9 cell even at 300 K), and the zero-temperature extrapolation does not remove the offset (the internal energy of the far hydrogen lies +0.82 to +0.96 eV above the fragments in the 3n cells at every temperature); Tier-2 sticking barriers are therefore computed at 300 K in the 10×10 cell, where the far hydrogen reproduces the fragments, and recombination barriers, referenced to the adsorbed state, keep the 3000 K free-energy protocol until the extrapolation is applied to their located structures. A single C–H reaction coordinate does not reach H₂ from the para pair or from the vacancy dangling-bond pair, so those recombination barriers come from bands ending in H₂ above the pair, and scan values that stop short of the product, rise to the end of the grid or do not converge are flagged (`converged = false`) in the barrier tables as path maxima rather than saddle points. At Tier 3 the ortho recombination bands on the (5,5) and (10,10) tubes do not converge: started from an IDPP path the (5,5) band ends at a flagged path maximum of 1.72 eV and the (10,10) climbing image leaves the recombination channel for the two separated hydrogen atoms (4.50 eV); seeded from the Tier-2 transition structures both bands stay in the recombination channel but still end unconverged after 600 steps at path maxima of 2.06 and 1.29 eV. The located Tier-2 saddles (2.46 and 2.62 eV) are the verify-tier tube recombination barriers and the Tier-3 tube values remain flagged.
- Tier-1 spin states are found from a symmetry-broken start (one Bohr magneton on every carbon with fewer than three carbon neighbours, alternating with the sublattice, total moment free) with the LCAO moments carried into the plane-wave single point. The first monovacancy and zigzag-edge anchor records, computed before this start was adopted, had plane-wave points in the non-magnetic state (the bare monovacancy, 1.40 μB in LCAO, 0.00 μB in the plane-wave point, formation energy 8.07 eV PBE; the hydrogen-terminated zigzag ribbon, 0.00 μB) and were withdrawn; the zigzag-edge anchor has been recomputed under the adopted start (antiferromagnetic edge state; edge C–H binding 0.19 eV stronger than the withdrawn value), the monovacancy anchor has been recomputed (magnetic substrate, 1.39 μB; formation energy 7.64 eV PBE inside the B11 interval, dangling-bond binding 0.43 eV weaker than the withdrawn value), and the spin-state check is in the manual's C2 section under Convergence and sensitivity.
- The Tier-1 Stone–Wales formation energy in the 6×6 anchor cell (5.41 eV PBE-D3, 5.29 eV PBE) lies 0.41 eV above the 5.0 eV reference of B11, and the benchmark fails at Tier 1. At Tier 2 the same quantity falls from 5.13 eV in the 6×6 cell to 4.84 eV in the 9×9 and 4.72 eV in the 12×12 cell, so the deviation is of the size of the cell bias of the 6×6 cell; a Tier-1 formation energy in a 9×9 cell decides the benchmark and is pending after the anchor set. Clean defect sheets relax planar at every tier because a planar start keeps its mirror symmetry; at Tier 2, relaxations of the Stone–Wales defect from out-of-plane seeds end within 0.02 eV above the planar energy, and the seeded test at Tier 1 is pending.
- The Tier-2 landscape is executed at both tiers under the revised protocol: 17 verify-tier configurations (9×9 sheets, armchair tubes of six unit cells at three diameters) and the full-tier enumeration of 125 configurations (12×12 sheets, eight diameters per chirality, edges, oxygen groups, mixed classes; no exclusions). Clean tube substrates are minimized along their radial breathing coordinate before relaxation: a wide tube rolled at the graphene bond length satisfies the per-atom force criterion unrelaxed while its hydrogenated configurations release the collective strain, which had shifted the exterior and interior binding energies of tubes wider than 1.2 nm by up to 0.34 eV in the first full-tier series; the recomputed series approaches the sheet value monotonically from both sides (at Tier 3 the unrelaxed tubes do not meet the force criterion, so the same step changes the verify-tier values by at most 0.004 eV). The equilibrium graphene bond length of each engine differs from the builder's 1.420 Å by about 0.4 % (1.414 Å for GFN2-xTB under the smearing protocol, 1.426 Å for MACE-MP-0), so the fixed-cell sheets carry that residual strain; it changes the basal-monomer binding energy by 0.016 eV at Tier 2 and 0.001 eV at Tier 3. Of the manual's Tier-1 convergence plan only the B1 acceptance subset (one cell size, one k-mesh, one cutoff and one vacuum step) has been run.
